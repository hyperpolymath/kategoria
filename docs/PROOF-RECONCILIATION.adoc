For independent *theory* statements the default Agda invocation is:

[source,shell]
----
agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible <module.agda>
----

This explicitly enables Cubical-compatibility support; it is not
the full `--cubical` feature. Where a genuine Cubical proof needs a
different profile, document the exception and its assumptions. No
Kategoria Agda/Lean/Coq correspondence has been verified by this note.

Coq/Rocq and Lean may independently reprove translated statements or
check suitably exported witnesses. Every claim of cross-prover
correspondence needs separate statements, assumptions and evidence.
Mathesis catalogues receipts; Kategoria retains its own research ownership.
